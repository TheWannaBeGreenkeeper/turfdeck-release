# TurfDeck

**Free Windows software for golf-course superintendents and turf managers.**

TurfDeck helps organize course records, track applications, and review weather information in one place. Built by a golf-course superintendent, it is being developed with feedback from people using it in their daily work.

## Free Public Beta

TurfDeck is free to use, with no required payment, subscription, or activation key. Optional donations support continued development and do not unlock additional features.

TurfDeck is a work in progress. Features and fixes will continue to improve based on tester feedback.

## Features

- Spray application logs with multiple products per tank mix.
- Fertilizer records, including pounds of product used and nutrient tracking.
- Course-area sizes and sprayer calibration records.
- Product inventory and application cost tracking.
- Course planning calendar.
- Weather forecasts, growing degree days (GDD), and reference evapotranspiration (ET₀).
- Historical weather estimates with automatic gap filling.
- Weather-based dollar spot monitoring.
- CSV exports and backup tools.

## Your Records

Course records are stored locally on your computer. Export a backup regularly, especially before updating or moving to another computer.

Weather features require an internet connection. Weather requests send the configured course coordinates to the weather provider.

## Weather and Management Guidance

Forecast data is provided by the National Weather Service. Historical weather estimates are provided by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). TurfDeck calculates GDD and summaries from weather data.

Historical estimates are regional reconstructions, not measurements from an on-course weather station.

Weather, disease monitoring, and application timing information are advisory. Always follow current product labels and use scouting, local conditions, and professional judgment.
